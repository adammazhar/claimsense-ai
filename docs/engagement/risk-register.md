# ClaimSense AI: Risk Register

Opened 2026-10-27 (Milestone 2). Reviewed every Saturday at milestone sign-off; a risk changes state
only with a dated line in its **Log** column. Scores: likelihood (L) and impact (I) are each 1 (low),
2 (medium) or 3 (high); exposure = L × I. Ordered by exposure, highest first.

Each risk names an **early-warning signal**: the first observable sign it is happening, with a date or
a threshold, so a risk is caught on the day it starts rather than at the milestone it breaks.
